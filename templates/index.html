Job Matcher

Score job listings from Adzuna & JSearch against your resume using Claude AI

How it works

  1. Upload your resume required — PDF, Word (.docx), or plain text (.txt)
  2. Set your search keywords optional — e.g. data analyst python — used to query both job boards
  3. Add soft preferences optional — location, salary, work type — these influence the AI score but never disqualify a job
  4. Click Run — jobs are fetched from Adzuna and JSearch, then Claude scores each one against your resume
  5. Download results — a CSV with all matches, scores, descriptions, and application links is saved when complete
